About TRACE

What this project does, who maintains it and where to find help.

Updated 27 October 2026

What you can do here

TRACE is Teja Priyan’s website intelligence tool. It fetches public pages to build an architectural map and inspect page content, links, metadata, technology signals and security headers. Its findings are clues to investigate. A crawl is a snapshot of what the server can retrieve, and cannot certify a website’s accessibility, security or search ranking.

Practical guidance

The guides explain common tasks and limitations. They are written for this project’s current behavior, rather than promising that every browser or device behaves identically. If an instruction does not match what you see, report the page and browser through Contact.
